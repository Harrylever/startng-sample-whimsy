Got a game? Put it on start.ng.

Four steps from your laptop to your own address on start.ng.

  1. STEP 1Build itAny static browser game: HTML, JavaScript, three.js, Phaser.
  2. STEP 2Push it to GitHubKeep the game in a public repository.
  3. STEP 3Publish itOne API call with your repo link, no key needed.
  4. STEP 4Approve it onceOpen the link you receive and your game goes live at its own subdomain. Every later push updates it.
